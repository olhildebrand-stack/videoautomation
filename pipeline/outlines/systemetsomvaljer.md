## HOOK
The claim, straight: everyone agrees AI handles the simple, repetitive things
and a human keeps the decisions. That is the part that is wrong, and this
system is why.

## THE PART EVERYONE KEEPS
Cutting, captions, colour -- the mechanical half automated years ago. What was
left by hand was choosing: which take survives, what the video is even about.
Judgement was the bottleneck, and everyone accepted it as permanent.

## IT CANNOT MAKE THINGS UP
Show the fence. The hooks come from a bank of lines that already worked, and
it is not allowed to write one -- if nothing fits it has to say so rather than
inventing something that sounds right. That refusal is the whole reason its
judgement can be trusted at all.

## IT GETS TOLD OFF
Show the check failing. When it decides badly the correction gets written down
as a rule, and it cannot make that mistake again. It is not a smarter model
each time -- it is the same model with more of my corrections in front of it.

## LANDING
The decisions in your business have this shape too: made on rules you have
never written down, by whoever has been there longest. That is what this is,
and it is buildable now.
