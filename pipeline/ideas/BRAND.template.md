# Who this is for and what it is about

The questionnaire, not the answers. Copy it to `BRAND.md` beside this file and
fill that copy in — `BRAND.md` is gitignored, because the answers are the
operator's business and the repository carries the tool. The idea stage reads
`BRAND.md`, and until it exists everything the stage makes is a guess about
somebody generic.

    cp pipeline/ideas/BRAND.template.md pipeline/ideas/BRAND.md

Fill it in conversation, not as a form. Every question below is something the
operator already knows without thinking — nothing here asks them to invent a
strategy, name an angle or pick a tone, because those are what the stage is
supposed to produce, not what it needs to start.

## 1. What do you actually do?

Not the positioning. The thing you spend the day doing, in the words you would
use to a friend.

## 2. Who is watching, and what do they already believe?

Both halves matter. Content that argues with what someone already believes
performs differently from content that confirms it, and the hook bank is
sorted by which of those it does.

## 3. What do you want someone to think after watching?

## 4. What can you actually show?

The honest inventory: a screen you can record, a product with a UI, results
worth screenshotting, a place worth filming — or is it you talking to camera.
This decides which formats are open and which ideas are unfilmable, so an
optimistic answer costs more than a modest one.

## 5. What have you posted that worked, and what died?

Both. What died is the more useful half and the half nobody writes down.

**Answer:**

## 6. What will you not post?

Subjects, claims, people, a language, a level of exposure. Cheaper to state
once than to catch in review every time.
