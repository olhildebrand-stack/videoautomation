# What is this video ABOUT? Two or three lines of plain prose.

The editing pipeline now makes the editorial decisions, not just the
mechanical ones. It reads a file describing who the content is for, matches
hooks from banks it is forbidden to invent from, and refuses an idea that
breaks its own rules. When it decides wrongly, the correction is written back
as a rule and it cannot repeat that mistake.

The point is not video editing. It is that AI can be trusted with judgement
work when it is fenced in and made to accumulate -- which is the thing a
Swedish business owner cannot picture until they have seen one.

Replaces: the part of the job everyone assumes has to stay human.
